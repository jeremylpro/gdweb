# Runtime World Management

The `LevelClass` in `src/world/Level.js` manages the lifecycle of game objects and environmental elements during gameplay. It handles spatial partitioning for performance, ground/ceiling tile recycling, and the execution of visual effects triggered by player progression.

## Section-Based Visibility Culling

To maintain performance, the world is divided into discrete sections of 400 pixels each [src/world/Level.js44-50](https://github.com/brokemutt/gdweb/blob/1c1d347a/src/world/Level.js#L44-L50) Only objects within the player's immediate vicinity are rendered or processed for collisions.

### Spatial Partitioning Logic

1. **Section Generation**: As objects are spawned via `_spawnLevelObjects`, they are assigned to a section index based on their X coordinate: `Math.floor(obj.x / 400)`[src/world/Level.js255-260](https://github.com/brokemutt/gdweb/blob/1c1d347a/src/world/Level.js#L255-L260)
2. **Container Management**: Each section has its own set of Phaser Containers. The `_sectionContainers` array stores pairs of containers for every 400px block: one for `BLEND_NORMAL` and one for `BLEND_ADD`[src/world/Level.js45](https://github.com/brokemutt/gdweb/blob/1c1d347a/src/world/Level.js#L45-L45)
3. **Dynamic Culling**: In the `update` loop, the system calculates the visible range of sections based on the current `cameraX`. It toggles the visibility of section containers that enter or exit the viewport [src/world/Level.js520-540](https://github.com/brokemutt/gdweb/blob/1c1d347a/src/world/Level.js#L520-L540)

### Visibility Flow

| Function | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| `_spawnLevelObjects` | Populates `_sections` and `_collisionSections` arrays. |
| `_updateVisibleSections` | Iterates through `_sectionContainers` to set `visible = true/false`. |
| `getNearbyObjects` | Returns objects from the current and adjacent sections for collision checks. |

## Ground Tile Recycling

The ground and ceiling are composed of repeating sprites that are recycled as the player moves forward to simulate an infinite floor.

- **Initialization**: `_buildGround` creates enough `groundSquare_01_001.png` tiles to cover the `SCREEN_WIDTH` plus a buffer [src/world/Level.js66-86](https://github.com/brokemutt/gdweb/blob/1c1d347a/src/world/Level.js#L66-L86)
- **Recycling Mechanism**: During `update`, tiles that fall behind the left edge of the screen are repositioned to the far right of the tile queue [src/world/Level.js490-510](https://github.com/brokemutt/gdweb/blob/1c1d347a/src/world/Level.js#L490-L510)
- **Ceiling/Floor Animation**: In "Fly Mode" (Ship), the ground and ceiling can animate their Y positions. The property `_groundTargetValue` stores the intended Y-offset, which is interpolated over time to shift the floor and ceiling boundaries [src/world/Level.js31-33](https://github.com/brokemutt/gdweb/blob/1c1d347a/src/world/Level.js#L31-L33)[src/world/Level.js470-485](https://github.com/brokemutt/gdweb/blob/1c1d347a/src/world/Level.js#L470-L485)

## Object Interaction and Effects

### Enter Effects

Objects can be configured to appear with specific animations when they enter the viewport. These are managed via `_enterEffectTriggers`[src/world/Level.js39-42](https://github.com/brokemutt/gdweb/blob/1c1d347a/src/world/Level.js#L39-L42)

- **Slide**: Objects slide into position from an offset.
- **Fade**: Objects alpha-interpolate from 0 to 1.
- **Scale**: Objects scale up from 0 to their target size.

### Color Triggers

The level checks for color triggers stored in `_colorTriggers`[src/world/Level.js36](https://github.com/brokemutt/gdweb/blob/1c1d347a/src/world/Level.js#L36-L36) As the player's X coordinate passes a trigger's X position, the `ColorManager` is called to transition the background or ground colors [src/world/Level.js560-575](https://github.com/brokemutt/gdweb/blob/1c1d347a/src/world/Level.js#L560-L575)

### End Portal Construction

The level's conclusion is marked by an end portal.

- **Detection**: The `endXPos` is determined during level parsing [src/world/Level.js21](https://github.com/brokemutt/gdweb/blob/1c1d347a/src/world/Level.js#L21-L21)
- **Construction**: When the player reaches the end, `_spawnLevelObjects` places the final goal assets, often involving a specific portal object definition from `LevelLoader.js`[src/world/LevelLoader.js192-210](https://github.com/brokemutt/gdweb/blob/1c1d347a/src/world/LevelLoader.js#L192-L210)
