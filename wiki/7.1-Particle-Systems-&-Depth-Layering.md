This page documents the implementation and management of visual effects (VFX) and particle systems within `gdweb`. It covers the various emitters used for player movement, environmental interactions, and UI transitions, as well as the standardized depth layering system used to maintain visual consistency across the game's rendering stack.

## Overview of Particle Emitters

The codebase utilizes Phaser 3's built-in particle manager to handle various gameplay and UI effects. Most emitters are initialized within the `PlayerClass` or `GameScene` and use frames from the `GJ_WebSheet` atlas, primarily `square.png`.

## Implementation Details

### Circle Effects and Tweens

Expanding circle effects (used for level completion or portal transitions) are implemented using Phaser Graphics and Tweens rather than the particle manager.

The function `emitCircleEffect` creates a `Phaser.GameObjects.Graphics` object at depth 55 [src/effects.js16-20](https://github.com/brokemutt/gdweb/blob/1c1d347a/src/effects.js#L16-L20) It uses a tween to animate a radius property (`r`) and an alpha property (`t`), clearing and redrawing the circle on every frame [src/effects.js33-45](https://github.com/brokemutt/gdweb/blob/1c1d347a/src/effects.js#L33-L45)

### Blend Modes

Two primary blend modes are used throughout the VFX system:

1. **`BLEND_ADD`**: Used for glow effects, particles, and the floor line to create a "light" effect where colors brighten as they overlap [src/constants.js6](https://github.com/brokemutt/gdweb/blob/1c1d347a/src/constants.js#L6-L6)
2. **`BLEND_NORMAL`**: Used for standard sprites and shadows (e.g., ground shadows at depth 22) [src/world/Level.js110](https://github.com/brokemutt/gdweb/blob/1c1d347a/src/world/Level.js#L110-L110)

## Key Functions

### `emitCircleEffect(scene, x, y, startRadius, endRadius, duration, filled, pingPong, color)`

[src/effects.js16-48](https://github.com/brokemutt/gdweb/blob/1c1d347a/src/effects.js#L16-L48)

- Creates a transient `Graphics` object.
- Uses `Quad.Out` easing for filled circles to simulate a smooth expansion.
- Automatically calls `destroy()` on the graphics object upon tween completion.

### `emitWinBurst(scene, squareColor, colorCircle)`

[src/effects.js51-93](https://github.com/brokemutt/gdweb/blob/1c1d347a/src/effects.js#L51-L93)

- Calculates a random viewport position within defined margins.
- Triggers one filled circle effect.
- Spawns a particle emitter with `stopAfter: 25` to create a one-shot explosion of squares.

### `StreakClass`

[src/player/PlayerRenderer.js8-111](https://github.com/brokemutt/gdweb/blob/1c1d347a/src/player/PlayerRenderer.js#L8-L111)

- Manages the motion trail (streak) behind the player.
- Uses a `Phaser.GameObjects.Graphics` object with `Phaser.BlendModes.ADD`.
- Maintains an internal array of points (`_pts`) and prunes them based on `fadeTime`.
- Draws segments between points with decreasing alpha to create the "fade" effect.
